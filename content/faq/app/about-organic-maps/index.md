Organic Maps is a free, open-source map and navigation app built for offline use. Download a region before you travel, then search for places, plan routes, and navigate without mobile data. It is useful for walking, hiking, cycling, driving, and exploring unfamiliar places.

The maps use OpenStreetMap, a worldwide map maintained by contributors. Detail and accuracy depend on what has been mapped in each area. Organic Maps has no ads and does not track your location for advertising or analytics.

## What is the relationship between Organic Maps and MAPS.ME? {#history-and-mapsme}

Organic Maps started from the open-source MAPS.ME code and was created with the involvement of MapsWithMe/MAPS.ME founders and contributors. Its public launch was announced in June 2021. Organic Maps is developed as a separate project focused on offline maps, privacy, and navigation.

You can transfer saved places and tracks using compatible exported files; see [moving from another app](@/faq/bookmarks/import-from-google-maps-and-mapsme/index.md).

## Why is it called Organic Maps? {#name-and-logo}

The name expresses the project's aim to provide maps made with care, without advertising or tracking.
