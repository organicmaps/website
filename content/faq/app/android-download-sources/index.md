Use the links on the [download page](@/_index.md) for Google Play, F-Droid, Huawei AppGallery, Accrescent, or official GitHub APK releases. Obtainium can help keep an official APK installation updated.

The Google Play build includes the world overview maps; other builds may download them on first launch. The official web APK uses a separate app identity, so it can coexist with the store app. Its saved places and settings are separate: [export your bookmarks and tracks](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md) before moving between installations. Some other store builds share an app identity but have different signing certificates, so switching may require uninstalling first.

F-Droid builds use microG's open-source location client; Web and Google Play builds use Google's client. Neither client installs Google Play services or microG's system service on your device. F-Droid can use satellite and other location providers available on the device, but location behavior and reliability can differ between builds, especially without Google Play services or on custom Android systems. For a missing or frozen position, see [location troubleshooting](@/faq/map/can-not-find-position/index.md).

F-Droid updates are usually available 3–7 days after the Web APK is published on GitHub. F-Droid builds and publishes independently, so the delay can be longer.
