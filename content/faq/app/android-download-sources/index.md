Use the links on the [download page](@/_index.md) for Google Play, F-Droid, Huawei AppGallery, Accrescent, or official GitHub APK releases. Obtainium can help keep an official APK installation updated.

The Google Play build includes the world overview maps; other builds may download them on first launch. The official web APK uses a separate app identity, so it can coexist with the store app. Its saved places and settings are separate: [export your bookmarks and tracks](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md) before moving between installations. Some other store builds share an app identity but have different signing certificates, so switching may require uninstalling first.

Location-service options depend on the build and device. F-Droid is not limited to satellite-only positioning: it can use the device's available location providers and offers optional Google Play location support on compatible devices. Store review and build schedules differ, so an update may appear at different times. There is no guaranteed F-Droid delay.
