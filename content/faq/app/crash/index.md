---
title: "What should I do if Organic Maps crashes or runs slowly?"
description: "Troubleshoot Organic Maps crashes and slow performance, check storage and protect your bookmarks before reinstalling."
updated: "2026-11-06"
taxonomies:
  faq: ["app"]
extra:
  order: 70
---

Update the app, restart it, and check free storage. Note whether the problem follows a particular action, map region, imported file, or Android storage location. If maps are on an SD card, test internal storage.

On Android, make sure the system WebView component is enabled and up to date if the failure involves screens that display web content. WebView and SD cards are possible causes, not explanations for every crash. [Export bookmarks and tracks](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md) before reinstalling or clearing app data. If the problem continues, [send a report](@/faq/app/report-a-bug/index.md) with steps to reproduce it.
