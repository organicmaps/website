---
title: "Can I make Organic Maps the default navigation app on my iPhone?"
description: "Set Organic Maps as your default navigation app on eligible iPhones and iPads and check Apple's regional requirements."
updated: "2026-11-06"
taxonomies:
  faq: ["app"]
extra:
  order: 90
---

Where Apple offers the setting, open **Settings → Apps → Default Apps → Navigation** and choose Organic Maps. Update iOS and Organic Maps if the option is missing.

Apple currently makes this choice available in the European Union on iPhone and iPad, and in Japan on iPhone. Availability depends on Apple's device, OS, and regional requirements; see [Apple's default-app guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121430). It affects supported location links and does not guarantee that every link inside another app will open Organic Maps.
