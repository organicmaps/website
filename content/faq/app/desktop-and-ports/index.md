Desktop builds are available for Linux and macOS. The Windows version is in development. The interface and feature set differ from the mobile apps. See the [Linux Flatpak instructions](@/faq/linux/how-to-use-flatpak/index.md) and the project's [release downloads](https://github.com/organicmaps/organicmaps/releases).

GTK, Qt Quick, Sailfish, and Flutter ports are also in development. These efforts will bring Organic Maps to additional platforms and interfaces; their availability and feature coverage vary.
