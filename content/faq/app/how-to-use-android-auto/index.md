Install Organic Maps from Google Play for the supported Android Auto setup. Connect your phone to a compatible car or head unit and open Organic Maps on the car display. Download the regions for your journey on the phone first.

Google's current requirements include Android 11 or later for wired Android Auto, with additional requirements for wireless connections. Check [Google's Android Auto requirements](https://support.google.com/androidauto/answer/6348019) for your phone and car. These requirements are separate from Organic Maps' Android 5 minimum. Android Auto and Android Automotive OS are different systems; support for a phone connection does not mean the app can be installed directly on every car.

See [offline trip preparation](@/faq/app/offline-maps/index.md) before leaving. If guidance is silent, check the [voice troubleshooting steps](@/faq/voice/cant-hear-instructions/index.md).
