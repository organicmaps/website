Yes. Once you have downloaded the necessary maps, you can view them, search, calculate routes, and navigate offline. Your phone can determine its position using satellites without mobile data, provided location services are enabled and the device has a satellite positioning receiver.

Before leaving, download every region along your route, including border regions, and download a voice for offline navigation if your device requires it. Open the route once and check it. A first location fix can take longer without an internet connection. Map downloads, uploading OpenStreetMap edits, cloud synchronization, and loading external websites need internet access. Experimental satellite imagery is a separate online feature.

## How do I download maps for a trip? {#download-maps}

Open **Download Maps** from the main menu and select the countries or regions you need. Download all regions along your journey, not just the starting point and destination. Large countries may be divided into several downloads.

Use Wi-Fi if you want to avoid mobile data charges, wait for downloads to finish, and check that you can view and search the destination with the network disconnected. Leave enough free storage for map updates as well as the downloaded maps.

Before your trip, [set up an offline Android voice](@/faq/voice/text-to-speech-android-tts/index.md) if needed and [plan your route](@/faq/map/how-to-create-a-route/index.md). If a download fails, follow the [map download checks](@/faq/map/cant-download-maps/index.md).
