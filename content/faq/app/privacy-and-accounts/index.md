Organic Maps is free to download and use. You do not need an Organic Maps account to download maps, search, navigate, or save bookmarks and tracks.

An OpenStreetMap account is needed to upload map edits from the app. Optional iCloud synchronization on iOS uses your Apple account. The project is supported by [donations](@/donate/index.md) and contributions.

## Does Organic Maps collect or share my location? {#privacy}

Organic Maps does not track your location or collect personal analytics. You can read the [privacy policy](@/privacy/index.md).

Bookmarks and recorded tracks are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud synchronization on iOS, they also synchronize through your iCloud account. Exporting or sharing a location sends the information you choose to the app or recipient you select. OpenStreetMap edits and notes are public contributions. Optional external services, such as a custom imagery server, receive the requests needed to provide their service.

To protect saved data when changing devices, see [backups and exports](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md) and [iCloud synchronization and recovery](@/faq/bookmarks/backup-and-sync/index.md).
