The goal of Organic Maps is to build a better version of Google Maps: a free, open-source map and navigation app that is easy to use, works offline, and respects your privacy. The project combines community-maintained maps with ongoing improvements to navigation, search, and information about places.

See [features in development](@/faq/app/project-goal-and-roadmap/index.md#features-in-development) and [planned features](@/faq/app/project-goal-and-roadmap/index.md#planned-features) for what is coming next.

## Which Organic Maps features are in development? {#features-in-development}

Several major features are being prepared for release:

- [Worldwide 3D terrain, hill shading, sea and ocean depths, elevation at any point, and detailed contour lines in meters and feet](@/faq/map/3d-terrain-and-ocean-depth/index.md)
- [KML polygon import, export, and rendering](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-import/index.md#kml-polygons)
- [Follow Track navigation](@/faq/navigation/navigate-gpx-track/index.md)

Other features in development include:

- Live public transport schedules
- More frequent, incremental map updates
- Better search
- A map style designer for customizing map appearance
- The Windows version
- GTK, Qt Quick, Sailfish, and Flutter ports

See [release news](/news/) for availability in published builds and opportunities to test new features.

## Which Organic Maps features are planned? {#planned-features}

Planned features include:

- Live traffic and accident/roadwork information
- Photos and reviews of places
- Satellite imagery served by Organic Maps that works out of the box
- Online backup and synchronization on Android using Google Drive, followed later by cross-platform synchronization
- Custom icons for bookmarks
- Street view
- Broader address coverage using additional sources such as OpenAddresses
- An [iOS SDK for embedding Organic Maps in other apps](@/faq/developers/sdk/index.md)
- …and more!

We can't promise exactly when a feature will be ready, but your [donations](@/donate/index.md) and [support](@/contribute/index.md) can help speed up development.
