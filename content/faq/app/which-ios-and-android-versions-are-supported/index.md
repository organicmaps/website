Current releases require iOS or iPadOS 15 or later, or Android 5.0 or later. Android devices also need compatible graphics support, such as OpenGL ES 3.0 or Vulkan. Check the official store listing for compatibility with your particular device.

Organic Maps can work on Android without Google Play services. Android Auto has separate, higher requirements; see [using Organic Maps in a car](@/faq/app/how-to-use-android-auto/index.md).
