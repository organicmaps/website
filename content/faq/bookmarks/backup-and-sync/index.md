On iOS, you can enable iCloud synchronization for bookmarks and tracks in Organic Maps settings. Use the same Apple account and enable iCloud access on the devices you want to synchronize. The app offers to export a backup when you enable the feature.

Online backup and synchronization for Android using Google Drive are planned, followed by cross-platform synchronization. Until those features are available, use [export](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md) and [import](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-import/index.md) to transfer data between Android and iPhone. Synchronization is not a substitute for a separate backup: deletions and changes can synchronize too.

## Will uninstalling Organic Maps delete my bookmarks? Can I recover deleted data? {#recover-data}

Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data can remove locally stored bookmarks and tracks. [Export a backup first](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md), including before switching Android installation sources. Downloaded maps can be downloaded again; your personal saved data needs a backup or a previously configured synchronization source.

On iOS, check **Recently Deleted Lists** in **Bookmarks and Tracks** to restore a deleted list, unless you permanently removed it. This is list recovery, not a promise that every individual deleted item can be restored. If you had iCloud synchronization enabled, check the synchronized data, but do not assume that deleted items remain there.

If you have already lost the data, look for earlier exports and device backups. Organic Maps does not keep a server copy of your local bookmarks that support can retrieve.
