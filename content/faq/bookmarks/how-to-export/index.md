To make a full backup, open **Bookmarks and Tracks** and tap **Export all Bookmarks and Tracks** to create one KMZ archive. Save the exported file outside Organic Maps, for example in your file storage or a cloud drive. Check that the file exists and can be opened before changing phones or uninstalling the app. For transfers between Organic Maps installations, KMZ is a useful default; different formats and receiving apps may preserve different details.

To share one place, open it and tap **Share**. To export a list, open **Bookmarks and Tracks**, use the list's menu, and choose an available format such as KMZ, GPX, or GeoJSON. Sharing a place link is different from exporting an entire list as a file.

To restore an export or move it to another device, [import the saved file](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-import/index.md). For automatic iCloud synchronization and deleted-list recovery, see [backup and sync](@/faq/bookmarks/backup-and-sync/index.md).
