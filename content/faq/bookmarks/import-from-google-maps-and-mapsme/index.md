updated: "2026-11-06"
taxonomies:

extra:

---

Export your saved places or tracks from the other app in a format Organic Maps supports, save the file to your device, and [import it](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-import/index.md). In MAPS.ME, look for each bookmark list's sharing or export option. Check the imported lists before removing the original app.

For Google Maps, current Organic Maps releases can import supported GeoJSON and Google Takeout **Saved Places.json** data. Google exports vary: a CSV or a different JSON structure may need conversion. A shared Google Maps route URL is not a GPX track or a full backup of your lists.
