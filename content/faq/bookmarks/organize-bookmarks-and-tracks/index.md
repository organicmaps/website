Tap a place or an empty point on the map and choose **Save**. To save exact coordinates, [search for them first](@/faq/search/places-and-coordinates/index.md#coordinates). Open the bookmark's edit screen to change its name, description, color, or list.

Open **Bookmarks and Tracks** to organize your saved items. You can move or delete an individual bookmark and use multi-selection to change the color, move, or delete several bookmarks and tracks at once. On Android, long-press an item or use the selection control; on iOS, use the list's selection controls. Deleting is different from hiding an item on the map.

[Export a backup](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md) before deleting items you might need again. If you have already deleted a list, see [recovery options](@/faq/bookmarks/backup-and-sync/index.md#recover-data).

## How do I move, edit, or hide a track? {#manage-tracks}

Open the track from **Bookmarks and Tracks** and use its editing controls to change its name, appearance, or list. Moving tracks between lists is supported on both Android and iOS. Multi-selection lets you organize several items together.

Use the eye icon to hide an individual track without deleting it. You can also hide a whole list. If imported data seems to be missing from the map, check these visibility controls first.

## Can I show bookmark names on the map? {#bookmark-labels}

Yes. Enable the bookmark-name display option in Organic Maps settings. Give your bookmarks short, useful names so the labels are easy to recognize. This is separate from showing or hiding a bookmark list.
