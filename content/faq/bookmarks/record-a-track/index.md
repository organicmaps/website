Track recording is available on both Android and iOS. Start recording from the app's track-recording control, allow the required location access, and stop recording when you finish. Save the track to keep it in **Bookmarks and Tracks**, where you can inspect or [export it](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md).

Recording uses your device's location and continues to need battery power even with the screen off. On current Android releases, you can also stop and save from the recording notification. Before a long trip, test that recording continues when your phone is locked.

If recording stops or has gaps, check [background recording and location permissions](@/faq/bookmarks/android-track-recording/index.md).
