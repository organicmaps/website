Track recording is available on both Android and iOS. Open the main menu and tap **Record Track**, then allow the required location access. When you finish, tap the recording indicator on the map to open its details, then tap **Save** to stop recording and keep the track in **Bookmarks and Tracks**, where you can inspect or [export it](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md).

Recording uses your device’s location and continues to need battery power even with the screen off. On Android, you can also tap **Stop and Save** in the recording notification or use the home-screen recording widget to start a recording and see elapsed time, distance, and elevation. Before a long trip, test that recording continues when your phone is locked.

While recording, the app updates a recovery backup once a minute in case it crashes. The latest points may not yet be in that backup, so save the finished track and export a separate backup for important journeys.

If recording stops or has gaps, check [background recording and location permissions](@/faq/bookmarks/android-track-recording/index.md).
