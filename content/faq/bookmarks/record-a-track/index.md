Track recording is available on both Android and iOS. Open the main menu and tap **Record Track**, then allow the required location access. When you finish, tap the recording indicator on the map to open its details, then tap **Save** to stop recording and keep the track in **Bookmarks and Tracks**, where you can inspect or [export it](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md).

Recording uses your device's location and continues to need battery power even with the screen off. On Android, you can also tap **Stop and Save** in the recording notification. Before a long trip, test that recording continues when your phone is locked.

If recording stops or has gaps, check [background recording and location permissions](@/faq/bookmarks/android-track-recording/index.md).
