The arrow can use the device's compass or its direction of movement. Magnets in cases or mounts and nearby metal can disturb the compass. Remove these sources, move away from interference, and follow your device manufacturer's compass-calibration guidance if necessary.

Update Organic Maps as well: recent releases include compass fixes. On iOS, compass calibration is handled automatically; the old Organic Maps calibration setting has been removed. An arrow pointing the wrong way does not necessarily mean that your GPS position is wrong.

## How do I keep north at the top or follow my direction? {#map-orientation}

Tap the compass to put north at the top. Use the location button to center on your position and switch between following your position and following your direction. Dragging the map lets you browse elsewhere; tap the location button to return to your position.

A rotating map can be normal in direction-following mode. If the heading jumps while the phone is still, see [compass troubleshooting](@/faq/location/compass-and-orientation/index.md).

If the position itself is wrong, follow the separate [GPS and location checks](@/faq/map/can-not-find-position/index.md).
