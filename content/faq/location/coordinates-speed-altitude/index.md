Tap your position marker on the map to open its details, including available coordinates, speed, and altitude. Use **Share** to send the selected position. Coordinate display options include decimal degrees and other supported formats.

These values depend on the location information supplied by your device; altitude and speed may be inaccurate with a weak signal.

## What does the direction arrow in a place's details do? {#direction-arrow}

When your location is known, the arrow indicates the direction and straight-line distance to the selected place. Tap it to open a larger direction display. This can help you orient yourself, but it does not calculate a walkable path around obstacles.

To enter a known location, [search for GPS coordinates](@/faq/search/places-and-coordinates/index.md#coordinates). To follow roads or paths to a place, [plan a navigation route](@/faq/map/how-to-create-a-route/index.md).
