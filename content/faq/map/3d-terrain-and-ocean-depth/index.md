Worldwide 3D terrain is in development; follow [PR #13952](https://github.com/organicmaps/organicmaps/pull/13952) for progress. It includes hill shading, the shape of the seabed and depths of seas and oceans, elevation information at any point on the map, and detailed contour lines (isolines) in meters and feet.

These features are being prepared for release. They make it easier to understand the terrain around a route and explore the landscape above and below sea level.

See [release news](/news/) for availability in published builds. For current outdoor map controls, see [hiking routes, cycling routes and elevation profiles](@/faq/map/hiking-and-cycling-routes/index.md).
