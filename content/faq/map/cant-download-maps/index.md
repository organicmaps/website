---
title: "What should I do if a map will not download or update?"
description: "Fix failed Organic Maps downloads and updates by checking storage, connections, network filters and Android map storage."
updated: "2026-11-06"
taxonomies:
  faq: ["map"]
extra:
  order: 20
---

1. Check your connection and free storage, then retry the download
2. Update the app and try a smaller region if you are downloading many maps
3. Try another Wi-Fi or mobile connection to isolate a network problem
4. Check whether a VPN, firewall, private DNS service, or network filter is blocking the download; compare with it temporarily disabled or with another connection
5. On Android, keep the app open while testing; if maps are on an SD card, try internal storage

If the problem continues, send the region name, exact error, app version, device, and connection type to [support](@/faq/app/report-a-bug/index.md). Do not delete the app or clear its storage before [exporting your bookmarks and tracks](@/faq/bookmarks/how-to-export/index.md).

See also: [updating the app and offline maps](@/faq/map/update-offline-maps/index.md) and [Android map storage](@/faq/map/map-storage/index.md).
