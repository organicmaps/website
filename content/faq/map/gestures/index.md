Drag with one finger to move the map. Pinch with two fingers to zoom and rotate with two fingers to change its orientation. Double-tap to zoom in.

A short tap selects a place or an empty point on the map. Long-press the map to hide or restore the interface. This is different from long-pressing an item in a bookmark list, which can select items for editing.

## How do I zoom with one hand? {#one-hand-zoom}

Double-tap the map, keep your finger down on the second tap, and move it up or down to zoom. On iOS, you can also hold a zoom button and drag.
