Open **Layers** at the top left and enable **Hiking Routes** or **Cycling Routes** to see mapped route networks. Tap a mapped route to inspect it and its available elevation profile. Availability depends on the region's data and zoom level.

The **Outdoors** map style is a separate setting that emphasizes outdoor features. Contour lines describe the shape of the terrain; walking and cycling route previews can also show an elevation profile.

Tap a mapped trail to inspect its available details. A line on the map does not guarantee current access or trail conditions. An imported track only has a useful elevation profile when elevation information is available.

## Can I see the elevation profile of a route? {#route-elevation}

Yes, walking and cycling route previews can show an elevation profile. Use it to understand the climbs and descents along the planned route. Current releases also warn about mapped obstacles such as steps and gates.

For imported or recorded tracks, available elevation information depends on the track data. A route preview and an imported track can therefore show different profiles. See [routes versus tracks](@/faq/navigation/navigate-gpx-track/index.md) for how to use each one.

For the development status of worldwide relief and more detailed contours, see [3D terrain and ocean-depth maps](@/faq/map/3d-terrain-and-ocean-depth/index.md).
