Tap a feature to open its details. Depending on the mapped information, you may see its type, name, address, opening hours, website, or Wikipedia article. Zoom in if nearby features are difficult to select.

Restoring hidden buttons is different from opening a map legend: long-press the map to restore the interface. Not every visual symbol or pattern is a separately selectable object.

## Why are downloaded regions purple? {#purple-regions}

The optional **Purple downloaded regions** setting highlights the areas for which you have downloaded maps. It shows download coverage, not terrain or a routing restriction. Turn the setting off if you prefer the normal map colors.

## Can I read Wikipedia articles offline? {#offline-wikipedia}

Some places include Wikipedia articles in the downloaded map data. Select the place and open its available description to read the included text offline. Article and language coverage varies; this is not a download of all Wikipedia. Following an external website link still needs internet access.
