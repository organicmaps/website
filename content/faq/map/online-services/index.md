Live traffic, accident and roadwork information, and photos and reviews of places are planned features. They are not yet available in the published release.

For trip planning today, you can import compatible bookmark and track files with descriptions and read the [Wikipedia articles included in map downloads](@/faq/map/map-display-and-details/index.md#offline-wikipedia). For public transport, see the separate [coverage and timetable answer](@/faq/navigation/public-transport/index.md).

See the [project roadmap](@/faq/app/project-goal-and-roadmap/index.md) for features in development and future plans.
