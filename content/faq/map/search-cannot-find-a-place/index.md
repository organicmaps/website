title: "Why can search not find a place or address?"
description: "Find missing places and addresses in Organic Maps by checking downloaded maps, search location, spelling and OpenStreetMap data."
updated: "2026-11-06"

Download or update the map for that area, move the map there, and try the place name with its town or country. Try a shorter query, another spelling, or a category. An address or business may be missing from OpenStreetMap or newer than your map download.

If you can see the object on the map but cannot find it through search, report its coordinates and the exact search text. If the object is missing from the underlying map, see [correcting map data](@/faq/editing/organic-maps-map-editing/index.md).

Better search is in development. Broader address coverage using additional sources such as OpenAddresses is also planned.

See [search formats and examples](@/faq/search/places-and-coordinates/index.md) for categories, coordinates and postcodes, or [report a search problem](@/faq/app/report-a-bug/index.md).
