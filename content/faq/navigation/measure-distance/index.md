---
title: "How do I measure a straight-line distance?"
description: "Use Organic Maps Ruler mode to measure straight-line distances and multiple segments without following roads."
updated: "2026-11-06"
taxonomies:
  faq: ["routes-and-navigation"]
extra:
  order: 50
---

Choose a point, tap **Route to**, and select the **Ruler** mode. Use **Route from** to choose a different start. Tap additional points to measure a line with several segments.

Ruler measurements do not follow roads and are not turn-by-turn routes. The distance in a place's direction display is also a straight-line distance.
