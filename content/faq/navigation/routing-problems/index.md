Check the travel mode, start and end points, [stop order](@/faq/map/how-to-create-a-route/index.md#route-stops), and [avoidance settings](@/faq/map/how-to-create-a-route/index.md#avoid-roads). [Update the app and every downloaded region](@/faq/map/update-offline-maps/index.md) along the route, including neighboring regions at borders. A missing region can prevent a useful route.

Routing can also be affected by missing connections, access tags, one-way restrictions, turn restrictions, or surface information in OpenStreetMap. [Report the exact start, destination, intermediate stops, travel mode, settings, and map-data version](@/faq/app/report-a-bug/index.md). A screenshot helps explain the unexpected section. A different result from another app does not by itself prove that either route is correct.

An imported or recorded track is a different kind of data: map updates will not repair gaps or jumps in the original track file. See [routes and tracks](@/faq/navigation/navigate-gpx-track/index.md).

## Can I choose an alternative or shorter route? {#alternative-routes}

Yes. Current releases can offer a shortest alternative alongside the fastest route. Select an alternative in the route preview to compare distance and estimated time. Different alternatives are not guaranteed for every route.

Route estimates depend on map data and the selected travel mode. Organic Maps does not use live road traffic, so the fastest estimate may differ from actual journey time or another app's recommendation.
