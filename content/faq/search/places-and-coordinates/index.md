Search for names, addresses, towns, coordinates, and types of places such as drinking water, toilets, pharmacies, bicycle parking, or campsites. You can type a category even if it is not one of the suggested category buttons.

Download the region first. To search farther away, move the map to that area or include the town or country in your query. Results depend on the information included in the downloaded map.

## How do I search for GPS coordinates? {#coordinates}

Enter the coordinates in the search field. For decimal degrees, use latitude first and longitude second, separated by a comma, with a dot as the decimal separator: `41.9142, 12.4849`.

Degrees/minutes/seconds and grid formats are also supported, including UTM, MGRS, British National Grid, Irish Grid, and Irish Transverse Mercator. Use the format's complete coordinate, including its zone or grid reference where needed. Check the result before saving it. Tap **Save** to bookmark that point.

## Can I search by postcode or ZIP code? {#postcode-search}

Yes, supported postcode data includes UK postcodes and US ZIP codes. Download the relevant region and enter the code in search. A postal-code result identifies an area or reference location; it is not necessarily an exact building entrance.

If a place or address is missing, try the [search troubleshooting steps](@/faq/map/search-cannot-find-a-place/index.md). After selecting a result, you can [save a bookmark](@/faq/bookmarks/organize-bookmarks-and-tracks/index.md) or [plan a route](@/faq/map/how-to-create-a-route/index.md).
