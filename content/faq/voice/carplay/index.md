title: "Does Organic Maps work with Apple CarPlay? Why is it always dark?"

Yes, Organic Maps supports CarPlay in compatible vehicles. Install it on your iPhone, download the maps you need, and connect the phone to the car. Current releases include CarPlay dashboard support.

If the map is always dark, check **Appearance** in CarPlay settings on the connected car display. Choosing **Automatic** rather than **Always Dark** allows the appearance to follow the car's day/night behavior. The phone app's theme is not the only setting that affects CarPlay.

See [offline trip preparation](@/faq/app/offline-maps/index.md) and [voice troubleshooting](@/faq/voice/cant-hear-instructions/index.md) for help before driving.
