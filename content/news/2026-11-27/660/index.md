Organic Maps is free, open-source, and built to respect your privacy. Proton shares our commitment to protecting users' privacy, and we've partnered to offer another way to support Organic Maps development and server maintenance.

If you were already considering a paid subscription to Proton VPN or another Proton service, you can also help fund Organic Maps. When you subscribe through {% <sponsored_link url="https://protonvpn.com/organicmaps"> %}our referral link{% </sponsored_link> %} ({{ <sponsored_link url="https://protonvpn.com/organicmaps" /> }}), Proton shares part of your payment with the project.

That contribution helps us develop new features, fix bugs, and cover the server and bandwidth costs of making offline maps available to everyone.

This is one more way to support Organic Maps alongside [direct donations](@/donate/index.md), [other ways to contribute](@/contribute/index.md), and spreading the word.

Thank you to everyone who helps us keep Organic Maps free, open-source, and respectful of your privacy. And thank you to Proton for supporting our work!
